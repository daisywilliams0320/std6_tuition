Learning Notes

Simple explanations suitable for Malaysian Year 6 pupils.

🌬️

What is air pressure?

Air pressure is the force exerted by air molecules on a surface.

Tekanan udara ialah daya yang dikenakan oleh molekul udara ke atas permukaan.

⚖️

Air has mass

Air is made of particles. Although we cannot see air, it has mass.

Udara mempunyai jisim.

📦

Air occupies space

Air fills containers such as balloons, bottles and tyres.

Udara memenuhi ruang.

↔️

Pressure acts in all directions

Air pushes on every surface from all sides.

Tekanan udara bertindak dari semua arah.

📉

Air moves from high to low pressure

Air flows from an area of higher pressure to an area of lower pressure.

Udara bergerak dari kawasan bertekanan tinggi ke kawasan bertekanan rendah.

Sealed Plastic Bottle

A student sees a sealed plastic bottle shrink after taking it out of a freezer.

Seorang murid mendapati botol plastik tertutup menjadi penyek selepas dikeluarkan dari peti sejuk beku.

❄️ 🧴 ⬅️➡️

When the air inside is cooled, its pressure becomes lower. The greater air pressure outside pushes the flexible bottle inward.

Suggested answer

Differences in air pressure can cause objects to move or change shape.

Perbezaan tekanan udara boleh menyebabkan objek bergerak atau berubah bentuk.

Suggested answer

The air inside the bottle is cooled. Its particles move more slowly and exert lower pressure than the air outside.

Udara di dalam botol disejukkan. Zarah-zarahnya bergerak lebih perlahan dan menghasilkan tekanan yang lebih rendah daripada udara di luar.

Suggested answer

The air particles move more slowly and collide with the bottle walls less often and with less force.

Zarah-zarah udara bergerak lebih perlahan dan berlanggar dengan dinding botol dengan kurang kerap serta kurang kuat.

Suggested answer

No. Air from outside can enter the open bottle and balance the air pressure. Therefore, the bottle will not be pushed inward.

Tidak. Udara dari luar boleh masuk ke dalam botol yang terbuka dan mengimbangi tekanan udara. Oleh itu, botol tidak akan ditekan ke dalam.

Suggested answer

Leave the cap loose or remove the cap before placing the bottle in the freezer so that air can move in and out.

Longgarkan atau tanggalkan penutup botol sebelum meletakkannya di dalam peti sejuk beku supaya udara boleh keluar dan masuk.
